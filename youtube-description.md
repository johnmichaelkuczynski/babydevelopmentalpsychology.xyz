**Basic Developmental Psychology** is a complete, self-paced introduction to developmental psychology — the science of how human beings change across the entire lifespan, from before birth through old age. It is built for serious learners coming to the discipline for the first time: graduate students, researchers, and working professionals who need a rigorous foothold in a new field.

This is a full unit of study, taught end to end by AI:

📚 Eight substantive topics — what developmental psychology is and how it is studied; nature vs. nurture; the infant mind; attachment; how thinking develops (Piaget); language development; the adolescent brain; and aging across the lifespan.
📖 Three-depth lessons — read any topic at Short, Medium, or Long depth, with the same examples and learning objectives preserved at every level.

📊 Live analytics and one-click operator diagnostics that verify the entire stack before you trust a session.
