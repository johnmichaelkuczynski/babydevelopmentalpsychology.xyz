## Full description

How do we become who we are? Basic Developmental Psychology is a self-paced, AI-taught course that covers the science of how people grow and change across the whole lifespan. It is a substantial, rigorous introduction built for serious adult learners — graduate students, researchers, and professionals entering the field.

An AI tutor teaches each lesson, answers your questions about the exact paragraph you are reading, drills you with practice that adapts as you go, and grades your homework with real written feedback. Every submission is screened for AI authorship — by a text classifier AND a keystroke-pattern detector — so the work stays honest.

One unit, eight topics:
• 1.1 What developmental psychology is
• 1.2 Nature vs. nurture
• 1.3 The infant mind
• 1.4 Attachment — the first bond
• 1.5 How thinking develops (Piaget)
• 1.6 Language acquisition
• 1.7 The teenage brain
• 1.8 Aging and the lifespan

What's inside:
• Three-depth lessons — read the Brief, Standard, or In-depth version of any topic
• A section-scoped AI tutor that streams answers in real time
• Adaptive practice that gets harder on a streak and easier after a miss
• AI-graded homework, a unit test, and a final — each with written feedback
• Two-layer AI-authorship detection on every answer you submit

Built for graduate students, researchers, instructors exploring AI-taught coursework, and academic-integrity researchers.
